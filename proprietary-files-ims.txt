## Not in the TheMuppets ether list: upstream drops the QTI IMS userspace because LineageOS
## disabled the pre-P IMS stack (bullhead 5cef16f). Kept here for VoLTE -- see VOLTE-BRINGUP.md.
## The QMI/RIL transport these talk to (libqmi*, libril-qc-qmi-1.so) is already in
## proprietary-files.txt and is NOT repeated here.
##
## The lib-rtp*/lib-dplmedia entries do not look like IMS and were missed by name-matching: they
## came from resolving what the daemons actually NEED, transitively. ims_rtp_daemon restart-looped
## with "CANNOT LINK EXECUTABLE ... library lib-rtpsl.so not found" until they were added, and
## lib-dplmedia is a second-order dependency that no name pattern would have caught. Resolve
## dependencies, do not grep names.
##
## STEP 4 SET ONLY: daemons, libraries and configs. The apps and the framework jars are commented
## out at the bottom -- they cannot be shipped as they are. ims.apk is 27 KB against a 2.3 MB
## ims.odex because its dex is stripped OUT into the odex, so importing the apk alone installs a
## shell with no code, and a 7.1 odex is meaningless against 13's boot image. Deodexing and
## rebuilding them is steps 1-3; until then they stay out of the image.
##
## They also cannot go in PRODUCT_COPY_FILES at all:
##   build/make/core/Makefile:72: error: Prebuilt apk found in PRODUCT_COPY_FILES
